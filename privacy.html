Your data stays on your device

Your budgets, accounts, and transactions are stored locally on your device. Pocklit has no account servers and never uploads your data to us. There is no sign-up and no user account.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync for a budget, that budget is stored in your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database) so it can sync across your devices. Sensitive details are encrypted on your device before they are sent, and only your devices hold the key. We cannot read this data, and it is never shared with us or anyone else. Local budgets on a Mac are also backed up to your own iCloud Drive. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Encryption at rest

You can optionally protect a budget with whole-database encryption. When enabled, the budget file is encrypted on disk and can only be opened with your password, Touch ID, or Face ID. The key is kept in your device's keychain. If you forget the password, we cannot recover the budget.

Permissions on iPhone

All of these are optional and are used only for the stated purpose, on your device:

  • Camera and Photos: attach a receipt image to a transaction. The image is stored with the budget (on device, or in your private iCloud if sync is on).
  • Location (while using the app): suggest nearby merchants while you add a transaction. Your location is not stored and not transmitted.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: unlock an encrypted budget. Biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave.

Imports and integrations

Bank statement files you import (CSV, OFX, QIF, MT940) and YNAB export files are read on your device. The MoneyMoney extension on the Mac hands a file from MoneyMoney to Pocklit on the same Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere.

No tracking

Pocklit does not track you across apps or websites and contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting or analytics code of our own. We collect no usage data and no device identifiers. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us anonymized crash logs; that data flows through Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The App Store privacy label for Pocklit therefore reads "Data Not Collected".

Children

Pocklit is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. Every piece of your data is in your own hands: delete the app, the budget files, or the iCloud data (Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage → Pocklit) and it is gone. You can contact us at the address above with any question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
