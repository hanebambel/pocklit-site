Give every euro a job.

A budgeting app for Mac and iPhone that keeps your finances in your own hands. Free.

Download for Mac

Notarized by Apple. Requires macOS 27 or later. Open the DMG and drag Pocklit to Applications. Also coming to the Mac App Store and, for iPhone, the App Store.

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-up, no servers of ours. Pocklit never uploads your data to us.
  • Your budgets, accounts and transactions are stored on your device.
  • Optional iCloud sync keeps Mac and iPhone in step through your own private iCloud. Sensitive details are encrypted before they leave your device.
  • Optional whole-database encryption per budget, unlocked with a password, Touch ID or Face ID.
  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers.

Budget the envelope way

  • Money in your accounts shows up as To Be Budgeted. Assign it to categories until nothing is left.
  • Available tells you what each category still has after this month's spending.
  • Quick budgets, recurring amounts, and goals for the things you are saving for.
  • Multiple budgets side by side.

Transactions without the chore

  • The payee field suggests categories from your own history.
  • Split transactions, transfers, credit cards, reconciliation against your statement.
  • Import CSV, OFX, QIF or MT940 statements with a preview that skips what you already have. Bring a budget over from a YNAB export.
  • On the Mac, send transactions straight from MoneyMoney with the bundled extension.

See where it went

Spending by category, trends over time, budget versus actual, net worth, top payees. Automatic backups every time you close a budget.

How to use it

The guide explains the method and every screen: accounts, categories, budgeting month by month, transactions, imports, reports, backups, encryption, and iCloud sync with the iPhone.

Mac and iPhone

Pocklit for Mac is the full app. Pocklit for iPhone is the companion: create a budget on your Mac, turn on iCloud sync, and enter spending on the go. The iPhone app ships through the App Store.
