Support

Pocklit is a hobby project, built and maintained by one person in his spare time. Support is personal and honest, but it can take a few days, sometimes longer. Thank you for your patience.

Pocklit has no account and no servers, so there is nothing we can look up on your behalf. Start with the guide. Most questions are answered inside the app: Help in the menu bar on the Mac, or Settings → Help on the iPhone.

Contact

info [at] pocklit [dot] app

When you write, say which version you use (Pocklit → About Pocklit on the Mac; Settings → About on the iPhone) and whether the budget uses iCloud sync. Please never send a budget file: it holds your financial data.

Downloads

  • Latest Mac version (DMG) · all releases
  • Mac App Store and App Store (iPhone): coming soon.

Installing the Mac download

The DMG is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so Gatekeeper opens it without warnings. Open the DMG, drag Pocklit to Applications, eject the DMG. Updates: download the new DMG and replace the app; your budgets are kept.

Mac-Download installieren

Die DMG ist mit einer Developer ID signiert und von Apple notarisiert; Gatekeeper öffnet sie ohne Warnung. DMG öffnen, Pocklit in „Programme" ziehen, DMG auswerfen. Updates: neue DMG laden und die App ersetzen; deine Budgets bleiben erhalten.
